1️⃣ Warm-up

BRITISH HOMES

Типы домов:

There are many types of houses in the UK. Some are big. Some are small. Some are in the city centre and some are in the countryside. Here are the main types of houses in the UK.

Detached

A detached house is on its own with a garden and a driveway. Detached houses are big and are usually in the suburbs. They are popular with families, but they are expensive.

Semi-detached

Semi-detached houses are two houses together with a garden. They are popular with families, too. There are semi-detached houses in the suburbs and in a lot of big towns.

Terraced

Terraced houses are houses in long rows. These houses are in city centres and in big towns. They are small and sometimes there is a small garden or a yard at the back.

Block of flats

In city centres, there are blocks of flats. These homes are on top of each other. Some blocks of flats are very tall with good views at the top. They are very popular with young people.

Bungalow

Bungalows are small houses with gardens. They have only got one floor. There are bungalows in small towns and in the suburbs.

Cottage

In the countryside, there are cottages. These houses are small with big gardens. They are very pretty.

  • detached house -> dom, który stoi samodzielnie
  • semi-detached house -> dom, który jest połączony z innym domem
  • terraced house -> domy w rzędzie
  • block of flats -> blok mieszkań
  • bungalow -> domek jednorodzinny
  • cottage -> mały domek wsiowy

Reading – Exercise 1

Прочитайте текст про дома в Великобритании и 👉 сопоставить 1–3 с a–c.

У нас есть:
  1. A detached house
  2. Semi-detached houses
  3. Blocks of flats
И три описания:
  1. are two houses together
  2. are homes on top of each other
  3. is expensive

Odpowiedź:

×
🔹 Detached house

В тексте сказано:

They are usually in the suburbs.

They are popular with families, but they are expensive.

👉 Значит подходит описание is expensive.


🔹 Semi-detached houses

В тексте:

Semi-detached houses are two houses together with a garden.

👉 Значит подходит описание are two houses together.


🔹 Blocks of flats

В тексте:

Blocks of flats are homes on top of each other.

👉 Значит подходит описание are homes on top of each other.


🔹 Итог:
  1. A detached house – is expensive
  2. Semi-detached houses – are two houses together
  3. Blocks of flats – are homes on top of each other

SB Exercise 2

🔹 Что нужно сделать?

Нужно решить, предложение:

  • R (right) – соответствует тексту
  • W (wrong) – противоречит тексту
  • DS (doesn’t say) – в тексте нет такой информации

Odpowiedź:

×
🔎 Разбираем по одному
1️⃣ Terraced houses have always got a garden in the back.

В тексте сказано:

They are small and sometimes there is a small garden or a yard at the back.

❗ Ключевое слово — sometimes (иногда).

В предложении — always (всегда).

Это противоречие.

👉 Значит, это W (wrong).


2️⃣ Blocks of flats are in city centres.

В тексте:

In city centres, there are blocks of flats.

Это совпадает полностью.

👉 Значит, это R (right).


3️⃣ Cottages are expensive houses.

В тексте:

In the countryside, there are cottages. These houses are small with big gardens. They are very pretty.

❗ В тексте сказано, что они very pretty, но не сказано, что они expensive.

👉 Значит, это DS (doesn’t say).


4️⃣ Bungalows have got two floors.

В тексте:

They have only got one floor.

❗ В предложении сказано, что у них два этажа, а в тексте сказано, что у них только один этаж.

Это противоречие.

👉 Значит, это W (wrong).

🔹 Итог:
  1. – W
  2. – R
  3. – DS
  4. – W

🔹 Часть 1 — Answer the questions

Odpowiedź:

×
1️⃣ What types of houses are there in city centres?

В тексте сказано:

  • Terraced houses are in city centres and big towns.
  • In city centres, there are blocks of flats.

✅ Ответ: -> There are terraced houses and blocks of flats in city centres.


2️⃣ What types of houses have got a garden?

Смотрим текст:

  • Detached houses → have a garden and a driveway
  • Semi-detached houses → have a garden
  • Bungalows → are small houses with gardens
  • Cottages → have big gardens
  • Terraced houses → sometimes have a small garden

Если спрашивают просто «have got a garden», обычно берём основные:

✅ Ответ: Detached houses, semi-detached houses, bungalows and cottages have got a garden.

(Можно добавить terraced houses sometimes have a garden.)
