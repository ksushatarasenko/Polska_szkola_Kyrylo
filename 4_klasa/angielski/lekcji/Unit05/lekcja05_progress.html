🍕 My Favourite Food Day

My name is Ben and I love food!
My favourite food is pizza because it is tasty.
I also like eating apples and strawberries.
I don’t like drinking cola every day because it is unhealthy.

On Sundays, I help my mum cook dinner.
First, we chop tomatoes and onions.
Then, we add cheese and chicken.
Finally, we bake the pizza.

I drink milk and water every day because they are healthy.
Fruit and vegetables give us vitamins and energy.
