📘 4e – Grammar

🧠 Adverbs of frequency (jak często?)

PL: To słowa, które mówią jak często coś robisz.

RU: Это слова "как часто".

  • always – zawsze (100%)
  • usually – zazwyczaj
  • often – często
  • sometimes – czasami
  • never – nigdy (0%)

💡 Rule: I always eat breakfast (przed czasownikiem)

🔹 1. Choose the correct adverb

  • 1. John (100%) walks to school → ?
  • 2. Helen (25%) watches TV → ?
  • 3. They (50%) do homework → ?
  • 4. He (0%) is late → ?
  • 5. My dad (75%) has lunch → ?
always
sometimes
often
never
usually

📌 Word order

PL: always/usually stoi przed czasownikiem

PL: ale po "is/am/are"

🔹 2. Put the words in order

  • 1. always / early / is / for work / He → ?
  • 2. She / late / sometimes / is → ?
  • 3. shower / He / often / takes / a / at night → ?
He is always early for work
She is sometimes late
He often takes a shower at night

⏰ at / on / in

  • at → godzina (at 7:00)
  • on → dzień (on Monday)
  • in → miesiąc / pora dnia (in July, in the morning)

💡 easy: at = godzina, on = dzień, in = dłuższy czas
