STREET FOOD AROUND THE WORLD

Do you ever feel hungry, but don’t have the time to eat at a restaurant? Then pick up some street food! It’s fast, delicious, and a great way to discover great flavours. Let’s find out about some popular street food from different countries.

A. Fish and Chips – England

A very popular street food in England is fish and chips. Vendors take the fish (usually cod or haddock) and cover it in batter before they fry it in very hot oil. Then, they use some special paper to wrap the fish and chips. People usually put salt and vinegar on their fish and chips. It’s really tasty!

B. Hot Dogs – USA

In cities in the USA, there are hot dog carts on almost every street. A hot dog is a sausage in a bun. The vendors usually steam the sausages, and warm the buns up in a special oven.

Customers then put their favourite toppings on their hot dog. Some popular toppings people add are mustard, ketchup, onions and relish. Many people also add some red pepper to make it spicy!

Самые важные фразовые глаголы

  • pick up = купить / взять быстро

    Например: Let’s pick up some street food.

  • find out = узнать

    Например: Let’s find out about street food.

  • warm out = разогреть

    Например: Vendors warm up the buns..

В английском есть “маленькие слова-хвостики”, которые меняют смысл.

Например:

  • warm = греть
  • warm up = разогревать

Это английские “слова-команды” из двух частей.

Как описывать еду

It’s spicy. = Она острая.

It’s tasty. = Она вкусная.

It’s popular. = Она популярная.

Punctuation Time

Знаки препинания

  • . point = точка

    Когда просто говорим. - I like tea.

  • ? = вопросительный знак

    Когда спрашиваем. - Do you eat fish?

  • ! = восклицательный знак

    ! эмоции - Когда радуемся или удивляемся. - I love eggs!
